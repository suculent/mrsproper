| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03-01 | Information Disclosure | session blob at rest → account takeover | mitigate | safeStorage (Keychain/DPAPI) encryption + 0o600 perms; never plaintext when unavailable; never logged (Task 1, RESEARCH Pitfall 1) |
| T-03-02 | Information Disclosure | session written in plaintext when keystore unavailable | mitigate | persistSession throws ENCRYPTION_UNAVAILABLE and writes nothing; restoreSession returns null → routes to login (Task 1, D-03/Pitfall 8) |

| T-03-04 | Denial of Service | FLOOD_WAIT during login appears as a frozen hang | mitigate | floodWaitSeconds + FloodWaitController surface a visible countdown (D-13); auth client floodSleepThreshold kept low in plan 04 so waits throw not silently sleep (Pitfall 5) |
